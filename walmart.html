Project Overview

A comprehensive end-to-end retail analytics project analyzing Walmart's historical sales data from 2010 to 2012. The project combines Advanced Excel, SQL, Python, Machine Learning and Tableau to evaluate retail performance, identify seasonal patterns, predict sales and communicate actionable business insights.

$6.74B Total Sales
421,570 Sales Records
45 Retail Stores
81 Unique Departments
