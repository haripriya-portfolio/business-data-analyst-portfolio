My Journey With Data.

A story of curiosity, discovery, and a genuine passion for turning numbers into meaning.

I've always loved the idea that numbers can tell stories. There's something exciting about exploring data, discovering patterns, and finding answers that aren't immediately obvious. That excitement is what turned my curiosity into a genuine passion for data analytics.

My journey began with Electronics and Communication Engineering, and my passion for data grew stronger when I started working at Xoriant Solutions in India. Later, as a Business Analyst at Capital One in the United States, I discovered how powerful data can be in solving real business problems and making a meaningful impact.

With master's degrees in Data Science and Business Analytics and 3.5+ years of experience, my love for data continues to grow. What excites me most is that every dataset brings a new challenge, a new discovery, and another opportunity to learn.
